Implemented a new `OptimalController` in [flow_autotts/controllers/optimal.py](/inspire/hdd/global_user/gongjingjing-25039/sywang/jkcai/FlowEvo_ocr/flow_autotts/controllers/optimal.py). The idea is a beta-scheduled scout -> prune -> finish -> confirm -> adaptive backward refine policy, with one new mechanism that was not in the recent best snapshot: child allocation now depends on preview score gaps and uncertainty, and the tail-budget pass prefers late confirmation first, then spends remaining target NFE on fully evaluated local children from the current best anchor.

I inspected the spec, the injected context pack, the current `optimal.py`, `baselines.py`, and the incumbent snapshot from `r0000`. From the compact frontier rows: `beta=0.0` had reward `0.358123` at `7` NFE, `0.25` had `0.591981` at `22`, `0.5` had `0.701755` at `35`, `0.75` had `0.776531` at `47`, and `1.0` had `0.807150` at `61`. Relative to the beta-matched ODE baseline, the incumbent was already above baseline at `0.0`, `0.25`, and `0.75`, but slightly below at `0.5` and `1.0`. The last rejected proposals made real behavioral changes, but the compact lessons showed the same failure mode: more branching/preview churn while still underspending target NFE. This round targets that weakness directly.

The exact beta schedule is monotone by construction: target NFE is `10, 20, 36, 48, 64` for betas `0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0`. Width also scales monotonically: roots `2, 2, 3, 4, 4`; survivors/confirm count increase with beta; backward child budget increases with beta; pruning gets gentler with beta; high beta alone enables bounded SDE scouting and mixed-noise refinement. `beta=1.0` cannot exceed `64` because every action is gated by `target_nfe <= min(env.budget, 64)` and all forward/preview/refinement actions check affordability before execution.

Underuse reflection against the injected targets:
- `beta=0.0`, target `10`: earlier incumbent underused at `7`, but that was justified only by reward, not comparability. I adjusted this upward with two scouts and one tail confirmation so it should stay near `10` without adding backward noise.
- `beta=0.25`, target `20`: incumbent already beat baseline at `22`; I adjusted the schedule to aim closer to `20` with limited refinement and one late-use pass.
- `beta=0.5`, target `36`: this was the clearest weak regime below baseline at `35`. I adjusted it to spend the missing compute on late confirmation plus selective backward children instead of extra early churn.
- `beta=0.75`, target `48`: incumbent was good at `47`; I kept the same overall style but made pruning gentler and added tail utilization, expecting it to stay close to target without collapsing diversity.
- `beta=1.0`, target `64`: incumbent trailed baseline at `61`; I adjusted it to use more confirmation/refinement and a tail fill loop, while the affordability gates keep it below or at `64`.

Checks run: `python -m py_compile flow_autotts/controllers/optimal.py` and a small fake-env dry run over betas `0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0`. In that dry run, realized NFE was `10, 20, 36, 46, 63`, which is close to the target curve and confirms termination and budget gating. Main risk: if the real env’s preview uncertainty is noisy or sparse, the gap/uncertainty child allocator may still overcommit to the current best anchor at high beta. The proposal is mainly meant to improve the weak `beta=0.5` and `beta=1.0` regions while protecting the already-strong `0.25` and `0.75` regimes.
